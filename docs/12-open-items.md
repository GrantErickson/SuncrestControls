# Open items

Music licensing is the one decision that could change what you buy. Personal streaming accounts are [generally licensed for private listening](https://programminglibrarian.org/node/1743), and the religious service exemption may not reach background lobby music. Have church leadership or counsel settle this before launch.

If you need a licensed route, [Soundtrack's Unlimited plan](https://dynamicmediamusic.com/syb/product/soundtrack-unlimited/) reportedly imports Spotify playlists. It would replace the WiiM with its own player, and playlist buttons would need a new plan. Confirm the details with the company, because this comes from a reseller.
